Format
Written to match Fonebrew's own BpmnArchive: five standard BPMN namespaces plus xmlns:aarso, isExecutable="false", one flat <aarso:meta> per node, and dc:Bounds per shape. Edges are drawn as straight lines on purpose — the format carries no di:waypoint, so bend points would be thrown away on the phone. Unknown aarso:meta attributes you import are kept and re-emitted.
