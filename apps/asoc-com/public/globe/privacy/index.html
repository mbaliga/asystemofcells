Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Globe Watchface is a watch face for Wear OS. This page explains what it does with your information. The answer is short: nothing.

The short version

No account. No ads. No analytics or tracking. No network access. Globe Watchface does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the watch face reads

Globe Watchface shows the time, drawn from your watch's own clock. It does not read your location, heart rate, steps, contacts, messages, or any other sensor or personal data, and it requests no permissions.

Your settings

You can choose a colorway, an outer ring style, and whether to show the oil-dome crystal effect. Wear OS stores those choices on your watch so the face looks the same next time. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them. If you uninstall the watch face, they are removed with it.

No network, no third parties

The watch face does not connect to the internet and contains no code that could send information anywhere. It includes no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting libraries, and it does not sell or share information with anyone.

Google Play

If you install Globe Watchface from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect its own information under Google's privacy policy. That is separate from the watch face, and we do not receive it.

Children

Globe Watchface collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. The current version always lives here.

Contact

Globe Watchface is made by A System of Cells. For questions about this policy, write to globe@asystemofcells.com.
