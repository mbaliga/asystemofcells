Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

Odyssey Live Wallpapers is a live wallpaper for Android (package com.asoc.odyssey), made by A System of Cells. This page describes exactly what the app does with data. It is short because the app does very little.

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no profile of you. The only data that ever leaves your device is an approximate latitude and longitude, and only if you turn on the optional live-weather feature — used solely to fetch a weather forecast.

What the app collects

Nothing at all, by default. On a fresh install the wallpaper generates its own weather procedurally and makes no network requests.

Approximate location — optional, and off unless you turn it on. If you enable “Use my location's real weather” in Settings, the app requests the Android ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION permission. When granted, it reads your device's last known approximate position — it does not switch on GPS or track you continuously — and uses it to look up current conditions.

You can use the live-weather feature without granting any location permission at all, by entering coordinates yourself under “Set location manually instead”.

What is sent off your device, and to whom

When live weather is enabled, the app sends your approximate latitude and longitude over HTTPS to the Open-Meteo weather API (api.open-meteo.com) to retrieve the current cloud cover, precipitation, wind speed and weather code for that position. This is a transfer of location data to a third party, so it is worth stating plainly rather than folding into a general "we may share data" clause.

Open-Meteo is an independent service and is not operated by us. Its own terms apply to that request: open-meteo.com/en/terms.

No other data is transmitted anywhere. There is no other network activity in the app.

What is stored, and where

The most recent latitude and longitude are cached in the app's private on-device storage (Android SharedPreferences) so the wallpaper does not have to ask the system for a position on every refresh. Your wallpaper settings are stored the same way.

All of this stays on your device. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. Uninstalling Odyssey deletes all of it.

Permissions, and why each exists

PermissionWhy
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATIONOptional. Only to look up local weather when you enable that feature.
INTERNETOptional in practice. Only used for the Open-Meteo forecast request.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEOptional visual effect: if you enable the "signal" reactive setting, losing connectivity rolls a fog bank across the scene. Reads connectivity status only.

Children

Odyssey is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
