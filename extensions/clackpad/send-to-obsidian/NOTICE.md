# Notice

"Send to Obsidian" is an **independent, unofficial community extension**. It is not affiliated with, authorised, sponsored or endorsed by Obsidian or its makers. The name is used only to describe what the extension works with: it sends text to your own Obsidian vault.

It contains no Obsidian code, logo, icon, font or other asset. It writes ordinary Markdown files into a folder you choose and opens `obsidian://` links; it does not use any private Obsidian interface.
